Case Study · 04

OneBanc — Wealth Concierge

A 1-week sprint to answer one question: what should wealth management feel like when the user is worth millions?

FinTech Design sprint Design system UX challenge
Role
UI/UX Designer
Duration
1-week design sprint
Platform
Mobile
Type
Recruitment challenge

Overview

Completed as a high-stakes recruitment assignment: define a luxury visual language for a wealth-management platform serving high-net-worth individuals — and prove it with a working prototype, in seven days.

The problem

Most fintech apps follow the same generic "neo-bank" aesthetic — bright gradients, playful illustrations, mass-market energy. That language actively works against the trust and exclusivity a premium wealth product needs to convey.

The approach

A rapid sprint on the highest-impact screens: Home Dashboard, AI-powered Insights, and Secure Vaults. A dark-emerald palette, sophisticated typography and restrained depth cues establish a "wealth concierge" feel — closer to a private bank than an app store.

Seven days, three moves

01

Market research

Analyzed luxury banking competitors to isolate the visual cues of exclusivity and security — what makes an interface feel expensive rather than merely dark.

02

System architecture

An atomic design system with reusable Auto Layout components, so every screen in the prototype stayed 100% consistent under sprint pressure.

03

Visual polish

An 8px grid and subtle depth treatments tuned for a premium feel — restraint as the luxury signal.

What came out

  • High-fidelity interactive prototype delivered within the strict 1-week deadline
  • A luxury aesthetic clearly distinct from mass-market banking apps
  • 20+ reusable components built with Figma Auto Layout
  • A foundation laid for a scalable fintech design system
